== Extrapolation, which is not a measurement ==
  Everything below is arithmetic on the per-day rate above, not something
  this soak observed. It was measured over 12 business dates at scale 0.002
  and 720x compression; a real day offers different volume, so read the
  per-posting figure above rather than this one when the volume differs.

  outbox_record          2002417 rows and 2.6 GB over 250 business days
  idempotency_record     2175875 rows and 1.3 GB over 250 business days

  Nothing prunes either table. The retention period is a regulatory
  question rather than an engineering one, and this package does not
  invent one - F-28 stays open with these figures attached.
