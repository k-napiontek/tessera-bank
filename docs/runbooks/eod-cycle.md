# Runbook: end-of-day cycle

> **STUB.** Outline only. Filled by **WP-05**.

The overnight batch cycle: what it does, when it runs, what each step produces, and what to do when a step fails. Written for an operator at 03:00, not for an engineer at a desk.

## Planned contents

- Purpose, schedule and expected duration
- Step graph: SORT, then ACCTPOST, then EODREPT, with inputs and outputs per step
- Normal completion: what success looks like, and the control totals to check
- Failure modes per step, with the diagnostic and the action for each
- Restart and recovery, including how to avoid applying a movement file twice
- Escalation path and when to wake someone
- Rejects: where they land and who works them
