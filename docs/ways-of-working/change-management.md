# Change management

> **STUB.** Outline only. Filled by **WP-18**.

How changes are classified, approved and scheduled. Adapted from ITIL practice as banks operate it.

## Planned contents

- Change classes: standard (pre-approved, low risk), normal (CAB reviewed), major (architecture review then CAB), emergency (retrospective review within 48 hours)
- The change record: what changed, why, risk assessment, test evidence, rollback plan, approvers
- Change Advisory Board: composition, cadence, what it actually decides
- Freeze calendar: month-end, quarter-end, year-end, regulatory reporting dates
- Emergency change procedure and its retrospective approval path
- Rollback and back-out planning as a precondition of approval, not an afterthought
