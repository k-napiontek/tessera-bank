# DORA control map

> **STUB.** Outline only. Filled by **WP-18**.

The EU Digital Operational Resilience Act, applicable since January 2025, mapped to artefacts that genuinely exist in this repository. Where a requirement is out of scope for a source repository, this document says so rather than inventing coverage.

## Planned contents

- ICT risk management framework - tech radar, ADRs, dependency policy, architecture review
- ICT incident management, classification and reporting - incident-management.md, severity model, worked RCA
- Digital operational resilience testing - test strategy, failure injection, recovery testing of the EOD cycle
- ICT third-party risk management - dependency policy, vendor component register
- Information and intelligence sharing - out of scope for a source repository, and recorded as such
- For each: the requirement, the artefact, and an honest status of covered / partial / out of scope
